# &#x20;A study examining the effects of reading the Bible more than 4 times a week.

# 

# The study is from the Center for Bible Engagement (founded in 2004), which researched the impact of Scripture reading on spiritual growth and behavior, commonly known as the "Power of 4" study.

# 

# Our Daily Bread Ministries

# &#x20;

# Key Details About the Study

# Organization: Center for Bible Engagement (often referenced alongside ministries like Back to the Bible or Our Daily Bread Ministries).

# 

# Back to the Bible

# &#x20;

# Core Finding: Engaging with the Bible four or more days a week shows a major shift in spiritual and emotional health, whereas reading 1 to 3 times a week yields little to no measurable difference compared to reading 0 days.

# 

# Impact Metrics (4+ days/week):

# Reductions:

# 1\. Lower rates of loneliness (\~30%)

# 2\. Anger (\~32%)

# 3\. Relational bitterness (\~40%)

# 4\. Alcohol abuse/excessive drinking (\~57%), 5. Pornography use (\~59%–61%)

# 6\. Spiritual stagnation (\~60%).

# 7\. Increases: Higher rates of sharing faith (\~228%)

# 8\. Discipling others (\~200%–230%)

# 9\. Scripture memorization (\~407%).

# The 260-Day New Testament Journey

### A 5-Day-a-Week Community Devotional Project

Welcome to your year-long journey through all 260 chapters of the New Testament. Designed for a 5-day-a-week reading habit (Monday through Friday), this project provides a consistent rhythm of scripture, relatable illustrations, and practical modern applications.
