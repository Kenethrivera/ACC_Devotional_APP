Devotion

A thought for today

Every day we face subtle and grand situations that require intentional decisions, deep patience, and quiet faith. Sometimes we feel the urge to understand every corner of the landscape before daring to take the next step.

Yet surrender calls us to acknowledge that human vision is inherently bounded. When the road ahead seems uncertain, we can seek His wisdom through prayer and His Word. True peace doesn’t spring from having the entire map, but from abiding in fellowship with the Guide.

Reflect on this question: What area of your life do you need to surrender into His capable hands today?
