Bible Passage

Proverbs 3:5-6
NIV ·

5 Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; 6 in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.

DEVOTION

A thought for today

Every day, we face situations that require decisions, patience, and faith. Sometimes, we want to understand everything before taking the next step. But God reminds us that trusting Him is not about having all the answers. It is about knowing that He is faithful.

Proverbs 3:5-6 teaches us to trust the Lord with all our hearts and not depend entirely on our own understanding.

When the road ahead seems uncertain, we can seek His wisdom through prayer and His Word.

Trusting God does not mean life will always be easy. It means choosing to follow Him even when circumstances are difficult.

As you walk through your schedule today, ask yourself: In what areas am I trying to maintain complete control? Where can I release outcomes into His gracious hands?
